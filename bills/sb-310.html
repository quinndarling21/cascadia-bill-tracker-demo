Summary

Lets anglers renew recreational fishing licenses online or by phone, adds an opt-in automatic renewal with an advance reminder, and creates a 15-day grace period with no late fee.

Full text

Sec. 1. Online and phone renewal. The Department of Fish and Wildlife shall allow a recreational fishing license to be renewed online or by phone at any time during the 60 days before it expires.

Sec. 2. Automatic renewal. A license holder may choose automatic annual renewal. The department shall send a reminder at least 30 days before each automatic renewal is charged.

Sec. 3. Grace period. A license renewed within 15 days after it expires is treated as continuously valid, and no late fee may be charged during that period.

Sec. 4. Effective date. This act takes effect March 1, 2027.
