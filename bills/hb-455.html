Summary

Full text

Sec. 1. Grant program. The Department of Commerce shall establish a competitive grant program to extend high-speed internet service to unserved and underserved rural areas of the state.

Sec. 2. Eligible applicants. Counties, cities, tribes, public utility districts, port districts, and nonprofit cooperatives may apply for grants under this act.

Sec. 3. Service standards. Each funded project must provide download speeds of at least 100 megabits per second and upload speeds of at least 20 megabits per second to every location it serves.

Sec. 4. Local match. A grant may not exceed 75 percent of total project cost, except that the department may waive the match for projects in economically distressed counties.

Sec. 5. Effective date. This act takes effect July 1, 2027.
