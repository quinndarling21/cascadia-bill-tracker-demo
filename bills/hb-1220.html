Full text

Sec. 1. Task force created. A seven-member task force on daylight saving time is created. The governor shall appoint members representing agriculture, schools, public health, transportation, and small business.

Sec. 2. Scope of study. The task force shall compare remaining on standard time year-round with observing daylight saving time year-round, including effects on sleep, traffic safety, energy use, and coordination with neighboring states.

Sec. 3. Recommendations. The task force shall deliver its findings and recommendations to the legislature by December 1, 2027, and expires on that date.

Sec. 4. Effective date. This act takes effect upon enactment.
